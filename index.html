Qualification points by rank (N = 0)

Qual pts = ceil( erfinv((N − 2R + 2) / (1.07·N)) × 7 / erfinv(1/1.07) + 9 )  ·  Alliance pts = 21 − alliance # (0 if none)  ·  Playoff pts = 80 / 2level (0 if none)  ·  Total = qual + alliance + playoff + award.

When sorting by total points, ties are broken by: award points → playoff points → alliance points → higher qualification rank (smaller rank number).

For advancement, teams marked advanced are skipped and do not use a slot. Of the remaining teams the top y are highlighted gold, and the next x − y are highlighted bronze.
